# 📖 Overview

AtmanSys Lead Intelligence is an internal AI-powered application developed to support business development for AtmanSys.

The project was created to automate one of the most time-consuming parts of running a software business: finding potential clients, understanding their needs and preparing personalized commercial proposals.

Instead of manually researching companies, the platform leverages Google's Gemini AI to generate business prospects, analyze possible operational bottlenecks and suggest software solutions that could provide value.

Although originally built as an internal tool, the architecture was designed as a scalable SaaS application.

---


# 🎯 The Problem

As the founder of AtmanSys, identifying potential clients manually quickly became repetitive and inefficient.

Researching businesses, understanding their challenges and writing personalized proposals required a significant amount of time before even making first contact.

I wanted to automate this process using Artificial Intelligence while keeping full control over the generated leads through an integrated CRM.

---

# 💡 The Solution

The platform leverages Google's Gemini AI to assist in discovering potential business opportunities.

Users simply specify a business category and a target location.

The AI generates a curated list of potential companies together with contextual information. The platform then analyzes each lead, identifies possible operational bottlenecks and proposes software or automation solutions tailored to each business.

Users can also manually create, edit and manage leads through the built-in CRM, allowing AI-generated and manually collected opportunities to coexist in a single workflow.

1. Select a business category.
2. Choose a target location.
3. Gemini AI generates potential companies.
4. Review the generated prospects.
5. Analyze business opportunities using AI.
6. Detect operational bottlenecks.
7. Generate tailored software solutions.
8. Save qualified leads into the CRM.
9. Generate personalized commercial proposals.

# 🧠 Technical Challenges

Some of the main technical challenges during development included:

- Designing AI prompts capable of generating relevant business prospects.
- Producing structured responses from large language models.
- Managing AI hallucinations and inconsistent outputs.
- Building reliable workflows around generative AI.
- Combining AI-generated leads with manually managed CRM data.
- Creating reusable prompt templates for different business sectors.
