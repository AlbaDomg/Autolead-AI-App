# 📖 Overview

AI Lead Intelligence Platform is an AI-powered SaaS application designed to automate the process of discovering, analyzing and qualifying potential clients for software development and automation services.

Instead of manually researching businesses, evaluating opportunities and writing personalized outreach messages, the platform leverages Google's Gemini AI to generate qualified leads, identify operational bottlenecks and recommend tailored software solutions.

The application also includes a built-in CRM, allowing users to manage both AI-generated and manually created leads from a single interface.

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Finding high-quality business opportunities is often a repetitive and time-consuming process.

Sales teams typically need to:

- Search for potential companies manually.
- Research each business individually.
- Identify operational challenges.
- Determine whether automation could provide value.
- Write personalized commercial proposals.
- Organize leads in spreadsheets or CRM systems.

Repeating this process for dozens or hundreds of companies quickly becomes inefficient.

---

# 💡 The Solution

The platform leverages Google's Gemini AI to assist in discovering potential business opportunities.

Users simply specify a business category and a target location.

The AI generates a curated list of potential companies together with contextual information. The platform then analyzes each lead, identifies possible operational bottlenecks and proposes software or automation solutions tailored to each business.

Users can also manually create, edit and manage leads through the built-in CRM, allowing AI-generated and manually collected opportunities to coexist in a single workflow.

# 🧠 Technical Challenges

Some of the main technical challenges during development included:

- Designing AI prompts capable of generating relevant business prospects.
- Producing structured responses from large language models.
- Managing AI hallucinations and inconsistent outputs.
- Building reliable workflows around generative AI.
- Combining AI-generated leads with manually managed CRM data.
- Creating reusable prompt templates for different business sectors.
